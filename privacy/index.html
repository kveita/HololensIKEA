Privacy Policy for HololensIKEA

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Data Collection

HololensIKEA does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

  • The internetClient capability is used to request the public IKEA product page entered by the user and to download the GLB model URL exposed by that page.
  • The microphone capability is used for optional on-device voice commands. Audio is processed by the HoloLens speech-recognition APIs and is not recorded or uploaded by this application.
  • HoloLens spatial tracking is used locally to place and manipulate holograms. Spatial maps, head pose, and hand or gaze input are not collected or transmitted by this application.

Data Sharing

The application does not send personal information to the developer or any analytics provider. Network requests go to the IKEA URL selected by the user and the model host referenced by that page. Those services may process requests according to their own privacy policies.

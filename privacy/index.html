Privacy Policy for HololensSatelliteViewer

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Data Collection

HololensSatelliteViewer does not access, collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • The location capability is used exclusively to compute satellite positions relative to the device — coordinates never leave the device.
  • The webcam capability is used exclusively for HoloLens spatial mapping and head tracking — no camera images are captured, recorded, or transmitted.
  • The microphone capability is used exclusively for HoloLens voice commands and spatial audio — no audio is recorded or transmitted.
  • The internetClient capability is used only to download public satellite orbital data (TLEs) from CelesTrak.org.

Data Sharing

No data of any kind is shared with third parties.
